Image Recognition Study

TSM Rebuttal — Human-Blocking Evaluation (ImageNet-1K)

Purpose: We are evaluating how well people can recognize image content after a privacy-protecting transformation is applied. This study takes approximately 5–8 minutes.

In this study, you will see images one at a time and select the category you think best describes each image. Some images may be processed/distorted — do your best to guess even when the image is unclear.
